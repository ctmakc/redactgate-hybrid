RedactGate Hybrid is a pass-through hatch for AI prompts, built for the #BuildHybridAI hackathon with Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference.

Gemini Nano runs in Chrome on your device, finds names, organizations, addresses and numbers in a document, and swaps each one for a token. Cloud Gemini (Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite) works with the tokens only. The answer comes back and the real values are filled in on the device. A local ledger shows exactly what left.

Live demo: https://redactgate-hybrid-2026.web.app
Code (Apache 2.0): https://github.com/ctmakc/redactgate-hybrid
